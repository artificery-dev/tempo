# Settings

Settings is the fourth entry on the dock. It is a tree of pages, each a
list of rows drawn as cards on the wallpaper, walked with the wheel like
every other list. This page follows the tree as you see it on the player:
how a row is moved, then each section in the order it appears, with the
rows that change something and the rows that are drawn greyed because
nothing on the player answers them yet. Your choices are saved as you make
them and are back after a restart.

## Moving a row

| Row | What the center does |
| --- | --- |
| A group, with a chevron | Opens its page. Menu comes back up. |
| A switch | Flips it. |
| A choice with two or three short answers on the row | Takes the wheel: turn to slide between the answers, then press the center or Menu to let go. |
| A choice with a page of answers | Opens the page; the current answer is ticked. Choosing one returns you to the row. |
| A slider or a stepped number | Takes the wheel: each click moves one step, a fast spin two. Press the center or Menu to let go. |
| A colour | Opens a grid of swatches with the chosen one ringed. |
| An action | Does it. A destructive action first opens a page with the question, Cancel, and the action again. |
| A reading | Shows a value and does nothing when pressed. |

A greyed row cannot be opened or moved. A row is greyed when another
setting rules it out, such as Dim Level while Dim After is Never, or when
the player has nothing behind it. The volume keys and the playback buttons
keep working while a row holds the wheel.

The top page is headed Playback, Interface and Device, then Apps &
Extensions and System.

## Sound

| Row | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Volume | The playback volume, 0 to 100 in steps of 5. |
| Output, On New Audio Device Detected | What happens when the sound could move between Bluetooth and the speaker or headphones: Switch, Ask, or Ignore. Switch is the default. |

Volume Step, Volume Limit, Mute on a Long Press, Output Device, Pause When
Unplugged, Keep Speaker Enabled, the Tone page with Equalizer, Custom Curve
and Balance, and Volume Leveling are greyed. Which output is in use follows
the headphone jack and Bluetooth by itself; see
[Connecting to the device](connecting.md).

## Playback

Repeat, Skip Step, Seek Step, Previous Restarts Track and Save Play History

### Wallpaper

Image lists the pictures in a folder named Wallpapers on the SD card, in
your home folder, or inside Pictures in your home folder, all as one list.
The wheel opens folders and Menu walks back up them. Beside each picture
are three discs, the primary, accent and neutral colours it would give the
interface, read in the background as the wheel rests on a row. Choosing a
picture takes it in as the wallpaper and returns you to the settings page.
With no pictures found, the page says where to put some.

Palette lists the readings of the current wallpaper, Best match first, each
with its three discs; only colour slots set to Auto follow it. Fit chooses
Contain, Cover or Center for a picture that is not the screen's shape.
Reset Wallpaper puts the original picture back.

a tap, Dock on a double tap, App Menu on a hold of Menu, and Force Restart,
as readings.

Wi-Fi and Bluetooth open the radio screens described in

## Storage

### Tempo Data Location

The media library's database and cache can live on the player or on the SD
card; device settings always stay on the player and media files are never
moved. The page shows Preferred as Internal or External and whether the
player is Using internal storage or Using external storage, with Internal
and External buttons. Choosing the other location asks Move library data to
the SD card? or Move library data to internal storage?, with Cancel and Move
data, then moves the data and restarts the player's services. If saved
library data already exists at the destination the page offers Use
existing, Move current data and Cancel. Should a move be interrupted, the
page reads Finish moving your media library with a Retry move button.

The first time a card is inserted, the same question arrives as a card over
whatever you are doing, titled SD Card Inserted: Use card keeps the library
data on the card so it travels with it, Use device keeps it on the player,
and Menu answers Use device. A card that already carries a library is
offered as it is. The question is asked once per card.

### The SD card

| Row | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Eject SD Card | Stop playback and safely eject the SD card? with Cancel and Eject card. The page finishes the library's work, unmounts the card and says You can now remove the SD card. If it fails, Retry eject and Resume library are offered. |
| Format SD Card | Erases the card and formats it as exFAT, after a second confirmation, Erase and format. Tempo Data Location must be Internal first; the page says so otherwise. |

Storage Usage, Card, Show Hidden Files and the Backups page are greyed.
Hidden files are shown or hidden from the Files app's own Options card.

## Apps & Extensions

Installed, Permissions, Sources, Update Extensions Automatically and Allow
Unsigned Extensions are greyed.

## System

### Time & Language

Time Zone opens a list of areas with UTC first, then the places within the
chosen area, each with the note the zone table keeps about it. Choosing a
place sets the clock and tells the Auto theme where to find sunrise and
sunset; the player starts on UTC. If the zone cannot be applied a notice
says so and the previous zone is kept. Clock Format chooses a 12 Hour or 24
Hour clock. Set Automatically, Date and Time, Date Format, First Day of the
Week and Language are greyed.

### Developer

| Row | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Developer Mode | On by default. Off, it greys the rows below it. |
| Browse Full Filesystem | Adds Root to the places the Files app starts from. |
| Frame Counter | A frames-per-second counter in the bottom right corner. |

Logs and Log Level are greyed.

### The rest of System

Privacy, Updates and Reset are greyed throughout. About opens a page that
is not written yet.

## Rows that are not shown

Some rows in the tree need a capability the player does not declare, and
are left out rather than greyed: Haptics and Haptic Feel, Sample Rate, USB
Mode, Battery History, Charge Limit, Deep Sleep After, Deep Sleep Mode,
Wake Sources and Erase Everything.
