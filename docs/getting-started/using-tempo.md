# Using the player

Tempo is driven entirely from the Y2's controls: the click wheel, the five
buttons on and around it, the two volume keys on the side and the power key.
Every screen is a list or a card that the wheel walks, and the same few
gestures mean the same things everywhere. This page is the grammar of those
controls, then the parts of the interface you meet first: the dock that
switches between apps, the menus, Now Playing, the letter picker for long
lists, and the notices that appear over everything.

## The controls

| Control | Press | Hold |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wheel | Turn clockwise to move down a list, anticlockwise to move up. A fast spin jumps further. | |
| Center | Select: open the highlighted item, play the highlighted song, throw a switch. | The item's other action, where it has one: rearranging a list, moving a dock icon. |
| Menu | Back one screen. At the top of an app it brings up the dock. | The current app's menu, where it has one. Held for one and a half seconds. |
| Previous | Previous track. | Seek back ten seconds. |
| Next | Next track. | Seek forward ten seconds. |
| Play | Play or pause. | Stop, and let go of the queue. |
| Volume up, volume down | One step of volume. | Keeps stepping while held. |
| Power | One tap puts the screen to sleep or wakes it. Two taps bring up the dock or put it away. | The Power dialog. The same hold closes it again. |

A hold is a separate word from a press: a button held past its threshold
never also counts as a press when it is released. The center, Previous, Next
and Play buttons become a hold after 600 ms; Menu and Power after one and a
half seconds. Taps of the power key are counted within 350 ms of each other.

Previous, Next and Play work from every screen, whatever is on it. The other
buttons speak to the screen you are on.

### Wheel feel

How far one click of the wheel carries is set in Settings, under Controls,
Wheel. Sensitivity is High by default, one click per row; Medium is two
clicks per row and Low is three, with a partial turn carried over so a stiff
wheel still arrives. Acceleration, on by default, lets a fast spin move by
pages and opens the letter picker described below.

### While the screen is asleep

After a period without input the screen dims, then goes dark; both waits are
set under Settings, Display, and are fifteen and thirty seconds by default.
Any control brings the light back while it is only dimmed. Once the screen is
dark the wheel and Menu say nothing, so a thumb on the wheel in a pocket does
not walk the menus blind. Pressing the center only wakes the screen and does
not activate anything. Previous, Next, Play, the volume keys and the power
key keep working. Waking shows exactly the screen you left.

## The Power dialog

Holding Power from anywhere opens a card titled Power with two options on a
rail: Restart and Power Off. The wheel slides the box between them, Previous
and Next step it, and the center performs whichever is under the box. Menu
does nothing on this card, so the reflex that leaves every other screen
cannot restart the player by accident; hold Power again to close it. The same
two actions are on the Power page in Settings, under Restart and Power Off.

## The dock

The dock is a row of icons along the bottom of the screen and the switcher
between the apps behind them. Bring it up with two taps of Power, or with
Menu at the top screen of an app. Four entries are always there, in order:
Home, Apps, Library and Settings. Files is pinned beside them to begin with,
and any app opened from the Apps page keeps its own place in the dock while
it runs.

While the dock is up, the apps step back into the middle of the screen and
turn past like covers as the box moves along the icons, the one under the
box flat in the middle and its neighbours angled to either side. The status
bar names the app under the box. Cover Flow, under Settings, Controls,
Navigation, Dock, turns the angled covers into flat pages.

| Control | In the dock |
| --- | --- |
| Wheel | Slides the box along the icons. |
| Previous, Next | Steps the box one icon. |
| Center | Opens the app under the box. |
| Menu | Puts the dock away. |
| Center held | Starts rearranging: the caption reads Turn to move · Select to place. Turn the wheel to carry the icon, press the center to drop it. |
| Menu held | A card of actions for the app under the box. |

The card offers Open, Pin to dock or Unpin from dock, Rearrange, Close app
and Cancel. Home, Apps, Library and Settings cannot be unpinned or closed.
Closing FM Radio from here also turns the receiver off. Pins and the order
of the icons are saved with your settings.

Every app on the dock keeps its own stack of screens, so switching away and
back returns you to where you were. Remember Last Page, under Navigation,
turns that off and sends an app back to its first screen when you leave it.
Open Dock on Back, under Dock, decides whether Menu at the top of an app
brings up the dock; with it off, the dock is only the double tap of Power.

## Menus

The Apps and Library entries open lists. A row with a chevron opens a list
of its own; a row without one opens a screen. Menu goes back up one level.

| Menu | Entries |
| --- | --- |
| Apps | Files, FM Radio, Store |
| Library | Music, Podcasts, Recordings, Audiobooks, Movies, Shows |
| Music | Playlists, Songs, Albums, Artists |

Default View, under Settings, Controls, Navigation, draws these lists as
rows or as a grid of icons that the wheel reads left to right and then down
a row. Wrap Navigation lets a click past the last row come back to the first.

On the Apps page, Menu opens a card for the highlighted app instead of going
back: Open, Pin to dock or Unpin from dock, and Cancel. Choosing an app here
opens it as its own entry on the dock. To leave Apps, bring up the dock with
two taps of Power and pick another entry.

On the Library page, holding the center starts rearranging the sections: the
title reads Moving followed by the section's name, the wheel carries the row,
and the center or Menu places it. The order is kept.

A menu entry whose screen does not exist yet opens a page that shows the
entry's place in the menu and the words nothing here yet - menu goes back.
Playlists and Store open that page.

### Music

Songs lists every song by title, with the artist to the right. Albums and
Artists open onto their contents; an artist's page starts with All Songs and
then the albums. Pressing the center on a song plays the list from that song
and takes you to Home. A section with nothing in it says so and points to
Settings, Library, where the folders are chosen.

### Files

Files starts with the places it can browse: Home, and SD Card while a card
is inserted. Opening a folder adds a column beside the one it came from, so
the trail of folders you walked through stays visible on the left while the
wheel drives the column on the right. Each folder column has a Back row and
an Options row above its first entry, one click up from where the wheel
starts. Options is a small card with Show hidden files, Columns, which
chooses between two columns and one, and Done. Menu closes the rightmost
column, and closes the app from the first.

### FM Radio

FM Radio plays on the Home screen. The dial shows the frequency in MHz over
a band with a dot on each favourite station and a status word: ON AIR,
PAUSED, SEEKING…, TUNING… or ERROR. A station that sends RDS shows its name
and text, and the reading shows Stereo or Mono and the signal in dBm. The
headphone cable is the antenna. If the receiver cannot be reached the screen
says FM receiver unavailable.

| Control | On the radio |
| --- | --- |
| Wheel | Tunes one tenth of a megahertz per click, half a megahertz on a fast spin, wrapping around the band. |
| Center | Marks or unmarks the frequency as a favourite. |
| Previous, Next | Jumps to the previous or next favourite. |
| Previous, Next held | Seeks down or up to the next station. |
| Play | Turns the receiver off and on. |
| Play held | Closes the radio. |
| Menu | Brings up the dock. |

Playing a song or a video closes the radio.

## Now Playing

Home is the Now Playing screen. Idle, it shows the wallpaper with the clock
in the middle, and both the center and Menu bring up the dock. While a song
plays, the artwork fills the top of the screen and a card along the bottom
carries the title, the artist and album, a progress bar and the position and
length. The clock moves up into the status bar where the page's name would
be. Clock, Clock While Playing and Artwork are under Settings, Appearance,
Home Screen; Artwork chooses Fit, Fill or Off.

While something plays, the wheel is the volume. Pressing the center switches
the wheel to seeking: the progress bar gains an outline and a thumb, and each
click moves five seconds, thirty on a fast spin. Pressing the center again
returns the wheel to the volume. Menu brings up the dock. A video plays on
the same screen, filling it, with the same card over the picture.

While the library is taking in new music, a line under the card counts the
files done against the files found.

## The status bar

The bar across the top names the page you are on, or shows the clock on Home
while something plays. At the right it shows, from left to right, an
SD-card activity glyph while the card is busy, the play state, Bluetooth,
Wi-Fi and the battery. Battery Icon, Battery Percentage, Wi-Fi Icon,
Bluetooth Icon, Playback Icon and Hide Inactive Icons are under Settings,
Appearance, Status Bar. The battery is drawn as a cell with five bars and a
bolt while charging.

## Scrolling by letter

In a list of thirty rows or more, keeping the wheel turning in one direction
opens the letter picker after 600 ms. The list dims and a large letter
appears in the middle of the screen; each click now moves one letter, up or
down. Press the center, or leave the wheel still for a second, and the list
lands with the first row of that letter at the top. Songs, albums, artists,
time zones, file names and the collections all have letters; songs, albums
and artists file The, A and An under the word that follows, and names that
do not start with a letter gather under #.

A list without letters, such as a long settings page, moves by pages
instead after half a second of turning, and by two pages after ten clicks.
Both behaviours belong to Acceleration under Settings, Controls, Wheel, and
are off when it is.

## Notices

Short notices appear in the middle of the screen for a moment and fade
again.

| Notice | When |
| --- | --- |
| A square card with a speaker glyph over a bar | The volume changes, from the keys, the wheel on Home, or a Bluetooth device. With a Bluetooth device connected the card names it and reads its level. |
| Speaker, Headphones, or a device's name | The sound moves to another output. |
| SD card inserted, SD card removed | A card comes or goes. |
| Could not set the time zone. Try again. | A time zone the player could not apply. |

Two questions also arrive as cards over the wheel. When a new card is
inserted for the first time, SD Card Inserted asks whether to keep the media
library data on the card, with Use card and Use device; see
[Settings](settings.md). When a Bluetooth device appears and On New Audio
Device Detected is set to Ask, a card asks Switch to the device's name, with
Switch and Keep Current; see [Connecting to the device](connecting.md).

The way these controls reach the software, from the wheel's pins to the
words the app hears, is in [Input](../porting/input.md).
